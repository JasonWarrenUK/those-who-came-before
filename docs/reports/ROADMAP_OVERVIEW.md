## Phase sequence

Each former milestone of the single MVP roadmap became a phase with thematic milestones. Only one
phase is active; later phases are stored as `archived` until the roadmap plugin gains a planned
state (goblin-scout 3.x), and each carries a gate standing in for the phase before it.

- **Foundation** (complete): Deno runtime, full type system, seeded PRNG, test infrastructure,
  Project Explorer shell.
- **Generation Pipeline** (active): Stack Migration; Structural Grammar; Plausibility; Materials;
  Decoration; Features & Classification; Description; Excavation & Provenance; Corpus & Scholars;
  Pipeline Integration. Stack Migration, added 2026-11-18 after Deno's move to Cloudflare (Deploy
  shuts ~April 2027, runtime development ends ~October 2027), gates every other open task.
- **World State & Integration**: World Generation; Stores; Real-World Integration.
- **Player Interface**: Inspector Components; Study Workspace.
- **Knowledge Model**: Observations; Inferences & Hypotheses; Document Library.
- **Lens System**: Lens Computation; Lens Channels; Lensed Presentation.
- **Contradictions**: Detection; Strain; Surfacing; Resolution; Contradiction Interface.
- **Persistence**: Serialisation & Schema; Storage & Saves.
- **Career & Publication**: Document Tradition; Venues & Dissemination; Reputation; Career & Terms.
- **NPC Systems**: Peer Review; NPC Interpretation & Social Channels; Relationships.
