Eight Ideas Behind the Mechanic

The mechanism pages describe a machine that runs: an artefact goes in, structure and materials and tags come out. These pages describe something that doesn't run yet: the interpretive lens, contradiction, documents, career, all with contracts that are written, reviewed and quotable. Where the mechanism strand points at src/lib/engine/, this one points at src/lib/types/: 2,238 lines across ten files, 92 exports, none of it wired to behaviour, all of it real enough to check.

What “designed” means here Nothing on this strand carries a shipped/partial/designed badge. Those badges answer “does it run”, which is the wrong question for a page whose entire subject is a design that hasn't run yet. Each page instead says plainly, in prose, which parts are a shipped type a reader can open and which are a doc's intention with no type behind it yet: the same honesty the badges give the mechanism strand, in the form this strand actually needs.

Reading order

Organised by idea, not by document number. Several of the strongest subjects span three or four of the fourteen design docs, and one of them, the fact that engine code never knows whose beliefs it's processing, doesn't appear as its own heading anywhere. Reading the docs in order would hide it. Each page below states its own one-line prerequisite; nothing requires having read the design docs themselves.

Needs nothing else on this site. 02 Five Channels of Filtering Misleading Without Ever Lying The lens as five distinct, individually auditable interventions on perception, one of which works by removing information, on a cap. 03 Relative vs Absolute Classification Whose Culture Is the Classifier Judging? A fixed threshold fired on 4.3% of one culture's output and 48% of another's. The fix, and the tag it cost. 04 The Engine Doesn’t Know Whose Beliefs These Are One Interface, Player and NPC Needs §2. NPC wrongness is just claims in the same container the player uses, which is what lets an NPC's error become the player's. 05 Contradictions Are Revealed by Consequences Not by Checking Answers Needs §3. Full ground truth exists and is deliberately never diffed against directly. Denial is a supported code path with a cost. 06 Immutable Documents Retraction Is a Social Act, Not a Delete A document freezes when it's seen, not when it's saved. Retraction cascades forward through lineage, by degree. 07 The Commitment Ratchet Why Publishing Makes You Blinder Needs §2. The object that measures your career success is the same object measuring how badly you're about to misread the next artefact. 08 Labels That Emerge Instead of Being Assigned Nobody Chooses a Category Needs §6. A document classifies itself from its own content, then a venue classifies it again. The gap between the two is the mechanic.

Why these eight and not fourteen

Docs 02 (pillars), 03 (systems map), 08 (architecture) and 11 (deferred decisions) are load-bearing across every page here rather than subjects of their own: the pillars get cited where they decide something, doc 11's rulings are the spine of §3 and §4, and doc 08 already has a home on the mechanism strand. A page per document would have produced several thin pages and buried the two best ideas inside them.
